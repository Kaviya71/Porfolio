AI Integrated Driver Monitoring System with Automatic Braking

The system uses a Zebronics FHD USB Webcam (30 FPS, 2MP CMOS Sensor) to continuously capture the driver’s facial features. Using Artificial Intelligence and computer vision techniques, the system monitors eye closure and facial behavior to detect signs of sleepiness. When the driver closes their eyes for a prolonged duration, the AI model identifies drowsiness and sends a signal to the Arduino UNO, which controls the motors through the L293D motor driver. The vehicle is then automatically stopped to prevent accidents. This system provides a low-cost and effective solution to enhance road safety by reducing accidents caused by driver fatigue.
